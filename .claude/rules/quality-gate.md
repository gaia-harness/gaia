# Quality Gate

Before any `git commit`, **read `wiki/decisions/Quality Gate.md` and run its steps as written; do not rely on a remembered gate.** Its value-set step runs on every commit, even one that skips the rest of the gate. The page is the source of truth for the steps, skip logic, and rationale. Fix all warnings before reporting. STOP and report before committing, except inside a workflow whose own instructions commit without stopping, such as the fix round of `wiki/concepts/Audit Round Procedure.md` (`#### The fix round: fixer, verifier, gate`); step 10 of the page names the rest. There the workflow's own checkpoint is the review point.
